How can a changed program find a better packing?

Program population and trials

A trial runs one unchanged program once, from one starting packing with a numerical random seed. It may contain many center-move steps. The three columns are three executions, not three Luna calls. Trial, seed and fitness explained.

Three common seeded starts; 80 requested search steps; 8-second hard cap per trial. In iterative refinement, freeze the previous best packings and re-evaluate parents and children from that same bank. Selection: median, then minimum, then stable program ID; top three complete programs.
