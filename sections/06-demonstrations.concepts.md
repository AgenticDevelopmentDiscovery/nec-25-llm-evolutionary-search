# 06-demonstrations — teaching spine

## Claims

- Both N=26 and N=32 reproduce Appendix B.12 mathematical formulations, not the unpublished engine.
- Three gated source proposals per task expose ancestry, external feedback and actual selection.
- Construction progress across changing banks differs from program quality on fixed validation conditions.

## Decisions

- Follow Chapter 10; preserve the shared Markdown, sequential reveals and Aggie Maroon design.
- Programs are evolved; circle arrangements are evaluated witnesses.
- Distinguish published problem formulations from our measured classroom results.
- Use exactly two published unit-square cases: N=26 and N=32; three gated Luna requests per case.
- Define the problem in the opening pair; begin Demo 1 with the source/evaluation sequence rather than repeating its definition.
- Compare programs within a common frozen packing bank; archive cumulative construction progress separately.

- After each completed demo, an explicit numerical-only comparison freezes initial/final source and reruns the deduplicated union on original starts. Same seeds/limits; separate initial/final tables and 240-second task; no evolutionary feedback. Original starts are not held-out validation.

## Open questions

- Which actual proposals improve or plateau in the bounded live run? Record results; do not predict them.

## Not doing

- No claim of reproducing the unpublished AlphaEvolve engine, a record construction or global optimality.
