# 04-fitness — teaching spine

## Claims

- Feasibility and completion precede eligible fitness; scores are recomputed independently.
- The primary scalar is negative median summed radii over three complete current-bank trials.
- A valid partial witness is distinct from complete program fitness; costs remain separate.
- A deliberately flawed sum-only scorer rewards an overlapping toy packing; the correct checker rejects it.
- Frozen-source evaluation on fresh starts tests transfer separately from geometric feasibility.

## Decisions

- Follow Chapter 10; preserve the shared Markdown, sequential reveals and Aggie Maroon design.
- Programs are evolved; circle arrangements are evaluated witnesses.
- Distinguish published problem formulations from our measured classroom results.
- Instructor suggested circle packing; literal reproduction of the original assigned demo is no longer a compliance target.
- A fixed valid construction is legitimate for a fixed instance; hard-coding alone is not evaluator exploitation.

## Open questions

- Three fresh seeds give limited post-search evidence; do not claim unseen-instance generalization.

## Not doing

- No claim of reproducing the unpublished AlphaEvolve engine, a record construction or global optimality.
