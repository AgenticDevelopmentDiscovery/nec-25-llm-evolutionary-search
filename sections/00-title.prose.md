# Agentic evolutionary frameworks

## LLM-Driven Evolutionary Search: Evolving packing programs {#title .title-frame data-seconds="20"}

::: {.question .title-only}
**How can a language model improve a search algorithm through measured feedback?**

ENGR 689 · Chapter 10 · Texas A&M University
:::

::: {.notes}
Today we will build the Chapter 10 evolutionary loop around a concrete mathematical task. We evolve programs that search for circle packings. The circles are the programs’ outputs; they provide evidence about program quality. We will use both unit-square examples from AlphaEvolve, with 26 and 32 circles. Their mathematical problems are reproduced; our short, local Luna experiment is a classroom implementation of the discovery loop, not the unpublished AlphaEvolve engine or a claim to reproduce its reported records.
:::
