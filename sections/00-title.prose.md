# Agentic evolutionary frameworks

## LLM-Driven Evolutionary Search: Evolving packing programs {#title .title-frame data-seconds="20"}

::: {.question .title-only}
**Can we improve the programs that find good circle packings?**

ENGR 689 · Chapter 10 · Texas A&M University
:::

::: {.notes}
First we will explain the circle-packing problem and what makes one arrangement better than another. Then we will use the evolutionary framework in Chapter 10 to improve programs that search for these arrangements. The two demonstrations use 26 and 32 circles. These are the published mathematical tasks from AlphaEvolve. Our local controller and short Luna runs are classroom implementations, not a reproduction of the unpublished AlphaEvolve engine or its reported records.
:::
